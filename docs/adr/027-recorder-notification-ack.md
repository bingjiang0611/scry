# 027: Advance notification ACK only across proven delivery or recorded absence

**Status:** Accepted (documents existing behavior)

A commit notification is not a Git hook or proof of downstream upload. No activated callback leaves records on disk; it is not an empty backlog. Keep the durable ACK separate from the visited/retry checkpoint. A missing ACK for the active callback identity is different from a known cursor at sequence zero. Hash canonical bytes, not a JSON re-serialization or the legacy-normalized read view. Identity-bound old checkpoints stay read-only; migrate old shared progress under the delivery lock during unscoped drain, accounting for retained dead letters. A scoped drain must not advance the shared ACK or implicitly migrate the old queue.

Deliver under the hook's time and attempt budgets. Deferred retries consume neither a callback attempt nor the chance to deliver later available records. Use bounded streaming metadata and limit diagnostic bodies separately from their count. Persist positive receipts for delivery across gaps. If a sequence still has a record but its outcome is missing, stop advancement there and preserve the already proven ACK prefix. Only a sequence with no record left can be crossed as absent; persist that fact before advancing. Count absences monotonically and compact them into a bounded range sample. A read-only status check may explain a hole without persisting an ACK.

A dead letter remains recoverable even if publishing its state was interrupted or its source later disappears. Replay preserves history, publishes the authorized replay marker durably, and only then removes active quarantine. A lost-outcome record may already have been delivered: only explicit replay of that sequence may attempt it again. Recovery scoped to a Provider/session reports unreadable checkpoints, records and receipts rather than guessing they are not in scope. A backlog that cannot be read is unknown. Report callback absence in structured results and diagnostics, not as an unstructured log message.
